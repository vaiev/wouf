# 0.2.0

From: 0.1.0

There are no upgrade steps for this release.

`.agents/team/config.ini` is kept as it is, and the upgrade never touches bot
storages or logins. Three things change without a config edit:

- Task classification: `team goal` and `project brief` take their class from
  `teamlib.classify`. The former words of `bin/project` are a floor, so a
  design, compare or research goal, one over three areas or one without paths
  gets `standard` (or `complex`) instead of a class without review, and a goal
  with a security word in its text or path names is `security`.
- The tier label: a runner without a `mid` model runs `mid` on `strong`, and the
  log and receipts write that as `mid->strong`, where 0.1.0 wrote `mid→strong`.
  A receipt with the old form is refused (`FAIL form`).
- The lead's guard: it also catches force pushes and deletes of main after
  git's global options, and `rm -rf` of `$HOME` in its quoted forms.

The keys new in 0.2.0 are optional and do nothing until you set them:
`usage` and `login_paths` in `[runner.*]`, `[class.<name>]` sections,
`[egress] private`, `[classify] security_words` and `[classify] keywords.<name>`.
Without `usage`, Claude Code gets `builtin:claude` and every other runner
reports its tokens as unknown, as before.
