# Code of conduct

Human for human. Treat people here the way you want to be treated: with respect, patience and good faith. Criticise ideas, not people.

Not accepted: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone else's private information, and sexualised language or imagery.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Report problems to the vaiev organization owners at <https://github.com/vaiev>. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and block participants who break these rules.
